FAQ

Common questions

Is Sharmaji Tools really free? +

Yes — every tool is free to use, with no account, no watermark, and no daily limits.

Do my files ever get uploaded anywhere? +

No. Every tool on Sharmaji Tools runs locally in your browser, so your files never leave your device.

How do I get my music distributed? +

Submit your original song through Sharmaji Musics with your poster, audio file and credits. Most releases go live across platforms within about three weeks.

Which platforms does Sharmaji Musics distribute to? +

Spotify, JioSaavn, YouTube Music, Apple Music, Amazon Music, SoundCloud and 15+ other streaming and social platforms.

How do I get in touch about either one? +

Use the contact page and pick Tools, Musics, or General — your message reaches the right inbox.
