Support

Everything you need to know before you upload.

Is distribution really free?

Yes. There's no fee to submit or distribute your song to our partner platforms — you upload, we review, and it goes out under your name.

What are the poster specs?

3000 × 3000px, a 1:1 square ratio, saved as a .jpg. The artwork should be clean — no promotional text, no watermark, and no company logos anywhere on it.

What are the audio specs?

MP3 format, 320kbps bitrate, 44.1kHz sample rate. Lower-quality exports get sent back for a re-upload, so it's worth exporting at full quality the first time.

How long until my song is live?

Once your upload passes review, your song goes live across all connected platforms within about 3 weeks.

Do I keep the rights to my song?

Yes. Distribution doesn't transfer ownership — the song stays yours. We ask every artist to confirm they hold the rights before we submit anything on their behalf.

Which platforms will my song be on?

Every partner on our Partners page — Spotify, JioSaavn, YouTube Music, Apple Music, Amazon Music and the rest of the list, plus the social platforms where songs get shared and shazam'd.

What if my poster or audio gets rejected?

We'll tell you exactly what needs fixing — usually a resolution, format, or sample rate issue — and you can re-upload the corrected file without starting the form over.

Can I update the details after uploading?

Reach out to support with your release ticket number and the correction — as long as review hasn't finished, we can usually update it directly.
