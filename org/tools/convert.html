    How this works

    The document is read and laid out as a page, then that page is captured into a PDF — all inside your browser. Headings, paragraphs, bold/italic, lists, tables and images carry over; very complex layouts (columns, text boxes, tracked changes) may not. Like Compress, the output PDF's text won't be selectable — it's a page image, not a text layer.
