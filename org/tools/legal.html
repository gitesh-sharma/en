Entry 1

Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 08, 2026

What this site collects

Sharmaji Tools does not require an account, and almost every tool — all PDF tools, plus the Calculator, HTML Live Editor, Age Calculator, Image to WebP and QR Code Generator — runs entirely inside your browser. Files and text you work with in those tools are read and processed on your own device — they are not uploaded to, or stored on, any server operated by this site.

The two exceptions: Grammar Checker and Translator

These two utilities work differently from the rest of the site: they send the text you enter to a third-party service — LanguageTool for grammar checking, MyMemory for translation — over the internet, so that text does leave your device for those two tools specifically. Neither tool is used elsewhere on the site. See each third party's own privacy policy for how they handle that data.

Cookies and analytics

As of this version, Sharmaji Tools does not use tracking cookies or any analytics or advertising service. If that changes in future — for example, to add basic visit analytics — this policy will be updated to say so before it happens, and details will also appear in the Cookies section below.

Third-party hosting

This site may be hosted on a third-party platform (such as GitHub Pages). That host may log standard technical information — like IP address and browser type — as part of normal web server operation. That logging is done by the host, not by Sharmaji Tools directly.
