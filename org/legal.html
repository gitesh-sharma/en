Privacy Policy +

This Privacy Policy explains what information Sharmaji Technology Media collects across its properties — Sharmaji Tools, Sharmaji Musics, and the Gitesh Sharma Blog — why we collect it, and what we do with it.

Sharmaji Tools

Data we do not collect

Every tool on Sharmaji Tools runs locally in your browser. When you merge, compress, convert, sign, watermark or otherwise process a PDF (or use one of the everyday utilities), the file is handled entirely on your own device using client-side code. It is never uploaded to, or received by, our servers — so we have no way to see, store or retain the content of any file you work with there. The only information generated is standard web-hosting activity (see "Hosting logs" below).

Sharmaji Musics

Data we collect

To submit a release through Sharmaji Musics you provide information we need in order to review and distribute it, including:

  • Release details — song title, singer, lyricist, music producer and recording company
  • Submitter details — the name and contact information of the person submitting the release
  • Files — a release poster image and the audio file itself

These files and details are stored using Google Drive and used to review, prepare and distribute your release to streaming and social platforms. Once a release goes live, its title, artist name and cover art become publicly visible on the platforms it is distributed to — that visibility is the point of the service, so please only submit details you're comfortable making public in that way.

Gitesh Sharma Blog

Reading the Blog

Reading blog posts doesn't require an account and doesn't involve us collecting any personal information beyond the standard hosting logs described below. If the Blog ever adds a way to comment or subscribe, this policy will be updated first to describe exactly what's collected and how.

All properties

Contact form data

If you use our contact page, we collect the name, email address, topic selection, optional reference and message you enter. Submissions are delivered to our inbox through a third-party form processor (FormSubmit) rather than stored in a database we manage directly.

Hosting logs

This site, and the Blog, are hosted on GitHub Pages. Like most web hosts, GitHub's infrastructure may automatically log standard technical information — such as IP address, browser type and request timestamps — for security and performance purposes. We don't separately collect or analyse this data ourselves.

Third-party services we rely on

  • GitHub Pages — hosts this website and the Blog
  • Google Drive — stores files submitted through Sharmaji Musics
  • FormSubmit — delivers messages sent through our contact form
  • Distribution platforms — Spotify, JioSaavn, YouTube Music, Hungama, Apple Music, Amazon Music, SoundCloud, Facebook, Instagram, WhatsApp, Tidal, Shazam, iHeartRadio, Pandora, 7Digital, Zing Mp3, KKBox and other partners a release is distributed to

Each of these operates under its own privacy policy once your information reaches them.

How we use your information

  • To provide, operate and improve Sharmaji Tools, Sharmaji Musics and the Blog
  • To review, prepare and distribute music submissions
  • To respond to messages sent through the contact page

Data retention

There's nothing for us to retain from your use of Sharmaji Tools, since file content never reaches us. For Sharmaji Musics, submission details and files are kept for as long as needed to distribute and manage the release. You can ask us to delete the information we hold by emailing info.giteshsharma@gmail.com — note that removing a release already live on a third-party platform depends on that platform's own takedown process and may take additional time.

Children's privacy

Our services are not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under that age.

Your rights

You can request access to, correction of, or deletion of the personal information we hold about you at any time by emailing info.giteshsharma@gmail.com.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top of this section will reflect the most recent revision.
